Scoring audit

The existing numbers are unvalidated legacy scores, not measured quality or confirmed test potential. A 78 is not demonstrably better than a 75: independent factor evidence was not recorded. The previous component breakdown was a mathematical split of the total and has been removed.

Legacy scores and the previous automated shortlist remain visible for reference only. New listings show To assess. Neither a missing item in this directory nor a keyword match establishes what Target actually carries. Product comparisons now analyze recorded prices, pack sizes and customer-review evidence separately.

Required review before a real recommendation: shopper demand with a dated source; a meaningful unmet need or improvement against live Target comparators; viable retail/PMU assumptions; repeat-use potential; and sample, claims and supply feasibility. Missing evidence stays unknown, not zero or an invented score.

Priority Test

A merchant-reviewed opportunity with credible shopper evidence, a meaningful unmet need or improvement, plausible economics and no critical launch blocker. Target not carrying an item is not sufficient by itself.

Strong Watch

A promising opportunity with specific unanswered questions—such as demand, live assortment, cost or quality testing. It is not approved for a test yet.

Benchmark

A relevant product used to compare price, format, claims and performance. This is a comparison role, not a low quality rating.

Not assessed

The listing is captured, but the required evidence has not been reviewed. It can still be compared and selected for further investigation.
